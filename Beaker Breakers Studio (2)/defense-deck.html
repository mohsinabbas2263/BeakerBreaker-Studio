INTRODUCTION
Bringing the chemistry lab
to every student's pocket.
A single institutional platform that unifies AI tutoring, gamified experiments, smartphone-based AR and formal safety certification — so schools can deliver real practical chemistry regardless of their physical resources.
🧪

Built for secondary schools

Curriculum-aligned virtual experiments, school-wide portal with role-based access for students, teachers and administrators.

🤖

AI + AR in one platform

Gemini-powered guidance and a 3D avatar assistant appear in AR through any ordinary smartphone camera — no special hardware.

🛡️

Safety-first by design

Four-tier safety training with quiz gates and a digital certificate — students unlock experiments only after demonstrating competency.

🎮

Gamified engagement

XP, badges, 8 rank levels, class leaderboards, streaks and missions keep students learning through play, not pressure.

PROBLEM STATEMENT
Most secondary schools cannot
deliver practical chemistry.
⚠️
Safety risks

Labs restrict junior students from hazardous reagents, blocking hands-on learning.

💰
High cost

Chemical reagents and glassware are expensive and often unavailable in low-resource schools.

⚖️
Legal barriers

Many hazardous substances are prohibited in schools, making real experiments impossible.

👀
Passive observation

Scarce lab equipment forces most students to watch rather than perform experiments.

🛡️
No safety training

There is no formal, measurable competency verification before students enter a lab.

📱
Gaps in virtual labs

Labster, PhET, PraxiLabs and OLabs lack AI, AR, gamification and certification — together.

80%

of secondary schools worldwide report inadequate practical chemistry delivery — and no existing virtual platform combines AI tutoring, AR, gamification and institution-wide safety certification in a single portal.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES
Six deliverables that define
the core product.
1
AI Guidance System

API-powered tutor that teaches step-by-step, detects errors in real time and gives instant corrective feedback.

2
4-Tier Safety Training

Interactive certification that gates experiment access — students must pass 80%+ per tier.

3
AI Readiness Predictor

Analyses performance history and advises teachers whether a student is ready for a real lab.

4
Gamification Engine

XP, 60+ badges, 8 rank levels, class leaderboards, weekly missions and streak tracking.

5
Augmented Reality Mode

Virtual experiments overlaid on the real environment using any ordinary smartphone camera.

6
Cross-platform Access

Responsive web app plus Flutter mobile app — usable on computers, tablets and entry-level phones.

FUNCTIONAL & NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
What it must do, and
how it must perform.

⚙️ Functional WHAT

  • F1Role-based auth for students, teachers and school admins (Sanctum).
  • F2Four-tier safety training with 5-question quizzes and a pass gate at 80%.
  • F3Experiment runner with step guidance and AI error detection.
  • F4AR experiment mode with 3D avatar assistant on mobile.
  • F5Gamification: XP, badges, ranks, leaderboards, missions.
  • F6Digital safety certificate generated on full completion.
  • F7Teacher dashboard — live analytics & AI readiness reports.

📐 Non-functional HOW

  • N1Responsive UI — desktop, tablet and phone down to 360px width.
  • N2Sub-2-second response on the standard portal; <5s for AI calls.
  • N3Role-based access control with encrypted credentials in transit.
  • N4Works on Android 7+ / iOS 11+ with ARCore or ARKit support.
  • N5Graceful degradation when the camera is blocked (simulated AR).
  • N6Accessible — WCAG AA colour contrast, large tap targets.
  • N7Scales to 1,000 concurrent students per school via Redis caching.
